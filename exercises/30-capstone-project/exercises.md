## F. Exercises Without Answers

**Difficulty:** Expert

**Problem.** Build a multi-agent AI knowledge management platform.

- **Requirements.** Implement the stages in the graph and verify the scenario with an automated test.
- **Input example.** Upload a PDF, ask a question, then approve.
- **Expected behavior.** The graph suspends and streams updates after approval.
- **Hint.** List the values each stage reads and writes before connecting the edges.
