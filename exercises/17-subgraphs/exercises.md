## F. Exercises Without Answers

**Difficulty:** Advanced

**Problem.** Research workflow with separate search, analysis, and summarization subgraphs.

- **Requirements.** Implement the stages in the graph and verify the scenario with an automated test.
- **Input example.** Research LangGraph.
- **Expected behavior.** Each child maps its result into a parent channel.
- **Hint.** List the values each stage reads and writes before connecting the edges.
